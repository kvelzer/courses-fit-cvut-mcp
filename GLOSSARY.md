# FIT Courses

## Language

**Course code**

The identifier of a catalogue entry, such as `BI-ML1.21`. Multiple entries can point to the same course website.

**Catalogue snapshot**

A collection of course entries published together. The all-courses snapshot and a semester snapshot can give different homepages for a course.

**Course homepage**

The address advertised for a course in a particular snapshot. It can lead to Courses, another service, or a language-specific section. Some entries have no homepage.

**Course page**

An individual page of teaching material within a course website. Its place in the navigation and its headings are specific to that website.

**Semester archive**

A published version of a course website associated with a semester such as `B232`. Availability varies by course.

**Attachment**

A file linked from a course page, such as a PDF slide deck or a downloadable assignment.
